# Conjunto de prompts finais

As 30 imagens foram produzidas individualmente com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, usando as imagens locais do site como referência.

## Base compartilhada

```text
Use case: ads-marketing ou infographic-diagram
Asset type: Instagram feed post, exact portrait 4:5, final 1080 x 1350
Style/medium: premium natural food photography combined with clean editorial social-media typography
Lighting/mood: warm diffused studio light, intimate, artisanal, premium but approachable
Color palette: cream #f6f4f0, coffee brown #6f4e37, black, kraft and natural cookie browns
Typography: elegant editorial serif similar to Fraunces plus clean geometric sans serif similar to Manrope
Constraints: exact 4:5 portrait; render all Portuguese text exactly; preserve product fidelity from the reference images; keep safe margins; no watermark; no invented third-party logos
Avoid: misspelled text, generic pink bakery aesthetic, excessive props, clutter, people
```
